## Design Context

### Users
The owner/developer manages an ESP32-C3 from a laptop or phone, frequently connected to an offline device hotspot. They need precise update progress, actionable failures, network configuration, logs, and recovery controls.

### Brand Personality
Technical, restrained, dependable. Correct operational state matters more than decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dense but readable developer console, charcoal surfaces, warm amber accents, restrained light theme. Flat tables and clearly separated sections. Bundle all assets. Use monospace only for operational data and logs.

### Design Principles
- Distinguish transfer completion from verified, healthy installation.
- Preserve access to recovery and network state on narrow screens.
- Surface failures with the operation and a concrete next step.
- Never rely on color alone or an Internet connection.
- Keep controls keyboard accessible and honor reduced motion.
