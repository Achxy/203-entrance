## Design Context

### Users
Room 203 visitors standing near a shared 160×128 ESP32 display. Their phones are controllers; the wall display owns menu and gameplay. Visitors may be on iPhone or Android, offline, unfamiliar with pairing, or joining a friend.

### Brand Personality
Playful, welcoming, precise. The user's requested colorful animated games and Neko mascot supply the arcade identity; connection and recovery instructions must stay short and clear.

### Aesthetic Direction
A tactile pocket arcade: warm ivory enclosure, ink typography, tomato-red actions, buttery yellow details, pixel game art and generous physical controls. A dark controller surface supports play without distracting from the display. Avoid dashboard cards, technical jargon and decorative status claims.

### Design Principles
- One next action at a time; show the real connection state.
- Big thumb targets, visible focus, non-color status cues and reduced-motion support.
- Keep gameplay local and authoritative on the display.
- Treat guests fairly with transparent turn/idle limits.
- Make failure recoverable without a page full of configuration.
