A home for every light.

Bring WiZ lights onto the room network, then adopt them here.

A home key lets our gateway sign its commands. It does not prove the bulbs reject other clients. Restrictions across the Wi-Fi network require both Allow only verified controls in WiZ and a physical check that unsigned commands are rejected. This site does not change that setting.

Put the light on the room Wi-Fi

Use the WiZ app to pair it to the room’s 2.4 GHz network and enable Allow local communication. Your choice of all or verified-only controls is left unchanged here.

The WiZ app may need Internet during setup. The room hotspot provides local control only; complete WiZ setup before discovery.

Import the WiZ local key

The room owner supplies the local-integration key below. It stays on the gateway and is used to sign our commands, not to change the bulb’s access policy.
